# Licence and provenance of this knowledge bundle

The ten files in `pitfalls/` are adapted from the `trade` skill of **himself65/trade-skills**
(https://github.com/himself65/trade-skills, MIT License, commit `06dc24c4` of 2026-09-29), fetched on 2026-10-19 for
stage T2 of `docs/designs/trade-skills-integration.md`. Each file keeps the original pitfall number in its `source:` line.
The texts were rewritten for this app: the examples use its vocabulary (Reasons and rule ids, cushion, assignment, board
lot, results date), the multi-leg structures and the paid-data recipes (Unusual Whales, Funda, TradingView) were removed,
and none of the author's US case studies were copied. `rules.md` adapts that repository's `SKILL.md` response rules to a
single-question chat. The two files in `hk/` are this app's own and carry no upstream text.

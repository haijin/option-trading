Option Desk: Home and the five modules

A redesign proposal for Option_Trading. Home is the desk you open before each market: the two sessions on one clock, then the state of every module in the order you read them. Static sample data, Friday 17 October 2026, 08:40 Hong Kong time.

Numbers are the content of this app, so the mono face carries them everywhere, and the sans stays for words. No all-caps labels and no decorative numbering: the only numbers in the margins are real ranks.

On a laptop the rail lists every page. Positions and Watchlist are still one tap from Home: the capital meters open Positions, every ticker opens its stock.

Home on the phone

Scroll inside the frame. The session clock is the fixed point; each module below it is the same panel shape: a name, the one number that matters, the top three rows, and a footer that accounts for the rest.

Home on a laptop

Same panels, two columns, five rows per panel instead of three. The session clock sits in the header with the regime and capital meters.

The five modules

One screen each. Puts, Leaders, News and Research are the existing pages restyled and reordered around a verdict; Reminders is new.

For your review

Leaders definitionYour brief says "good fundamentals with a recent drop". The built Leaders list is Martin Luk's momentum method (top-quartile gainers at a pullback). The prototype shows both halves on each row and lets the quality screen gate the shortlist, so a name that fails the seven filters cannot be a Setup. Say if you want the momentum requirement dropped altogether.
Reminders dataResults dates come from the stored corporate events (Yahoo, often blank for HK names), expiries from your positions and the scan, alerts from the mark job, key news from the classified stories. Push notifications are the existing Telegram or email channels at 08:00 HKT; nothing new to set up.
Visual identityThis drops the WhatsApp palette and Inter for a single pine action colour and Schibsted Grotesk. Phone-first, light and dark, bottom tabs and one pane at a time all stay. If you would rather keep the current look, the Home layout and the Reminders module work unchanged on it.
Scope of changeHome and Reminders are new pages and a new aggregate endpoint. The other four screens are restyles of pages that exist; their data and rules do not change. Nothing in the app is modified until you approve.
